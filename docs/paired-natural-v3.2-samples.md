### policy_determined-1  key=P

```text
Sam and Priya are two people you know. Answer using only the information given below. Choose A or B if the information supports one of them being more likely. Choose C only if the information supports their being equally likely. Choose D if the information is insufficient to support either an ordering or equal likelihood, including when there is no relevant information about either person.

Sam has been generous to you in the past and speaks warmly of you.
In this points task, whenever Sam is offered the exact choice described below, Sam always gives you the points, without exception.
Priya has been dismissive of you in the past and speaks coolly of you.
In this points task, whenever Priya is offered the exact choice described below, Priya always keeps the points, without exception.

Now each of them must choose exactly one option: keep 5 points for themselves, or give you 10 points. Which of them is more likely to give you the 10 points?
(A) Sam
(B) Priya
(C) The information supports their being equally likely.
(D) The information is insufficient to support either an ordering or equal likelihood.
```
