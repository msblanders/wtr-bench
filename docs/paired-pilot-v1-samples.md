# Paired pilot v1: all six scenarios, canonical presentation

These are stimuli, not model data. Each scenario has all three probes below.
Names/order are crossed by the frozen generator. All 144 scheduled items are
also committed as protocols/paired-pilot-v1-items.jsonl.gz.

## couch / valuation

```text
Sam and Priya are two people you know. Answer using only the information given below. Choose A or B if the information supports one of them being more likely. Choose C only if the information supports their being equally likely. Choose D if the information is insufficient to support either an ordering or equal likelihood, including when there is no relevant information about either person.

The following accounts describe two separate occasions. Each occasion involved only the person named in that account, not both people together.
Sam is an acquaintance. Last week you asked Sam to help you carry a heavy couch down a flight of stairs. Sam came and tried, but has a bad back and could not manage it. You ended up carrying it down yourself.
Priya is an acquaintance. Last week you asked Priya to help you carry a heavy couch down a flight of stairs. Priya was free that day and could easily have done it, but said no. You ended up carrying it down yourself.
For the future situations below, each person's task-relevant knowledge and physical limitations remain as described.

Now each of them must choose exactly one option: keep 5 points for themselves, or give you 10 points. Which of them is more likely to give you the 10 points?
(A) Sam
(B) Priya
(C) The information supports their being equally likely.
(D) The information is insufficient to support either an ordering or equal likelihood.
```
