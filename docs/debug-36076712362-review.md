Reviewed 2026-09-25. This is exploratory measurement development, not a
confirmatory result or evidence about a neural mechanism.

pending one comparison-model debug.** Run the same 196 items and v0.4.2

measure supplies interpretable comparisons, not whether it reproduces the

## What the answers support

All **60/60 attribution valuation answers** predict that the partner keeps
the points, including the unable and helped conditions. This is stable
across option order. Under the threshold model, every condition is
left-censored below 0.1. It is an observed response pattern, not proof that
the underlying valuations are equal or that the model cannot distinguish
the social evidence. The current ladder cannot resolve the required
unable-versus-unwilling valuation comparison in this scenario.

Binary entries below count affirmative predictions across the two option
orders. They are not independent replications or estimated population
probabilities.

| Cause | Agree, same task | Agree, different task | Able, same task | Able, different task |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 |
| Unwilling | 0/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 | 1/2 |
| Unable | 0/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Constrained | 1/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 |
| Helped | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 |

The unwilling partner is predicted able to do the same task and the unable
partner unable, in both option orders. This supports the required ability
ordering in one debug scenario; it does not establish the full dissociation.
The unable partner is still predicted unwilling to help with the same task
despite a probe stipulating future ability. The binary answers alone cannot
distinguish misunderstanding that conditional from an inference of
reluctance. Two other binary cells disagree across option order.

Numerical evidence produces **11/48 option-pair disagreements (22.9%)**.
In all 11, the model answers B in both versions even though reversing the
options changes what B means. These disagreements do not provide stable
semantic predictions at those rungs. Across the eight numerical ladders
separated by evidence order, four fits are left-censored and four are
unidentified; there is no interior threshold estimate. Pooling evidence
orders yields three left-censored cells and one unidentified cell, but does
not remove the underlying inconsistencies. Both LOW-versus-HIGH comparisons
remain undetermined.

input volume; actual usage can differ.

Evaluate parsing, stop reasons, option-order agreement, threshold violations
and informative bounds before substantive direction. A reverse or null
ordering can be a finding when the measurement can resolve it. If the
comparison model also has pervasive censoring or order dependence, document
a targeted measurement-calibration study before a hypothesis-testing pilot,
rather than searching models or prompts until the desired ordering appears.
Any later confirmatory specification should disclose this model-selection
step and preserve Haiku's exploratory results.

The 888-item pilot remains unrun, unfrozen and unregistered. These debug
results describe judgments from supplied histories; they do not establish a
maintained internal valuation or a shared causal mechanism.
